## Status

## Direction

Warm Intelligence is Nexora's warm, content-led alternative to Signal Atelier
and Luminous Grid. It uses paper and stone surfaces, forest actions, restrained
terracotta support, and a relaxed editorial reading rhythm. Its signature is a
layered knowledge folio: source and version depth are presented as a calm stack
of paper rather than a signal rail or a modular control grid.

The generated public-home concept demonstrates the direction through:

- an empathetic, sequential reading path — Bring the note, Keep its context,
  Share with care — rather than dashboard density or technical latticework;
- visible planned-concept, fixture, not-live, no-connected-sources, and
  tenant-boundary guidance; and
- a 375px collapse specification: the header condenses, the folio becomes a
  vertical sequence, and all content becomes one readable column. The single
  desktop capture does **not** verify that mobile rendering.
